## Test Objective

Verify two AF focus fixes on Pixel 9a:

1. **v2.12.0-08** — Focus frame no longer degenerates into a square when face bbox is small
2. **v2.12.0-09** — No spurious 2s backoff when CameraX internally cancels a metering operation

## Test Cases

### TC-01: Focus Frame AR — Rear Camera, Face Far Away (v2.12.0-08)

**Steps**:
1. Open app, switch to rear camera
2. Point at a face 2-3 meters away (face should appear small)
3. Tap on the face to trigger Eye-AF

**Expected**:
- Focus frame is slightly rectangular (taller than wide, matching face proportions)
- Focus frame is NOT a perfect square

**Fail condition**: Frame is exactly square (48x48dp)

---

### TC-02: Focus Frame AR — Front Camera, Face Normal Distance (v2.12.0-08)

**Steps**:
1. Switch to front camera
2. Hold phone at arm's length (face fills about 20-30% of frame)
3. Tap face to focus

**Expected**:
- Focus frame is slightly taller than wide (portrait face shape)
- Not a perfect square

---

### TC-03: Focus Frame AR — Rear Camera, Face Close Up (v2.12.0-08)

**Steps**:
1. Rear camera, face 30-50cm from lens
2. Tap face

**Expected**:
- Focus frame is large (close to 200dp max), preserving face AR
- Not a square

---

### TC-04: No Backoff on Rapid Taps — Rear Camera Face (v2.12.0-09)

**Steps**:
1. Rear camera, point at a face
2. Tap focus rapidly 5-6 times in quick succession (every ~0.5s)

**Expected**:
- App responds to each tap without freezing
- No 2-second pause between taps
- Logcat shows `[v2.12.0-09] OperationCanceledException — reset to Idle (no backoff)` for cancelled operations

**Fail condition**: App pauses ~2s after rapid taps (backoff triggered)

---

### TC-05: Normal AF Still Works — Rear Camera (Regression)

**Steps**:
1. Rear camera, slow deliberate tap on face

**Expected**:
- Focus indicator appears, locks (green), then fades to dwell
- Logcat: `Focus successful at (x, y)`
- No regression in normal AF behavior

---

### TC-06: Front Camera Fixed-Focus Bypass (Regression)

**Steps**:
1. Switch to front camera
2. Tap anywhere to focus

**Expected**:
- Logcat: `[v2.12.0-07] Fixed-focus lens — AE/AWB metering only`
- No `FAILED` state in logcat
- Focus indicator shows as locked (no endless failure loop)
