## Analysis

### 1. **SmartCameraApp is MORE Efficient Than Google Camera** ✅

Despite being a 3rd-party app, SmartCameraApp uses significantly less Graphics memory than Google's official Camera app.

**Why?**
- ✅ Controlled resolution (800x600 vs Google Camera's higher resolution)
- ✅ ResolutionSelector optimization working correctly
- ✅ BitmapPool reuse (99% hit rate)
- ✅ Minimal UseCase configuration (Preview + ImageAnalysis only)
- ✅ No HDR/advanced features overhead

**Google Camera overhead**:
- ❌ Multiple resolutions/modes active simultaneously
- ❌ HDR+ processing buffers
- ❌ Night Sight pre-allocated buffers
- ❌ Magic Eraser / Photo Unblur buffers
- ❌ Video stabilization buffers
- ❌ All features "ready" even if not used
