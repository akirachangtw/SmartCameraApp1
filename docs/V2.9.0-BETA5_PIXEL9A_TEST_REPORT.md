## Technical Notes

### Why Pixel 9a Supports More Resolutions Than Sony

**Pixel 9a (29 resolutions)**:
- Modern Tensor G4 ISP (Image Signal Processor)
- Flexible resolution support
- Multiple aspect ratios (4:3, 16:9, 1:1, etc.)
- Hardware designed for computational photography

**Sony XQ-BT52 (21 resolutions)**:
- Older Snapdragon ISP
- More limited 4:3 resolution options
- Camera specialist but conservative resolution list
- Focus on image quality over flexibility

### Why Filter Range Matters

**beta4 Plan D (≤800x600)**:
- Too restrictive
- Forces CameraX into tight buffer management
- May cause more memory allocation overhead

**beta5 (≤1280x720)**:
- More flexibility for CameraX
- Better internal optimization possible
- Still filters out 1080p and higher (the real memory hogs)
