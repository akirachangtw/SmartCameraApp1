# v2.8.4 Quick Win Final Results - SUCCESS ✅

## Executive Summary

**✅ Target Achieved**: v2.8.4 Quick Win optimization **successfully achieved the FPS target (≥26)** under normal lighting conditions.

**Key Finding**: Initial test showing 16.7 FPS was caused by **insufficient ambient lighting**, not code performance issues. Camera hardware automatically reduces frame rate in low-light conditions to improve image quality.

## Critical Discovery: Lighting-Dependent FPS

### Controlled Lighting Experiment

| Lighting Condition | FPS | Status |
|-------------------|-----|--------|
| **Desk lamp ON** | **>29 FPS** | ✅ **Exceeds target** |
| **Desk lamp OFF** | **16.9 FPS** | ⚠️ Hardware auto-throttle |
| **Normal room lighting** | **27.9-28.8 FPS** | ✅ **Exceeds target** |

**Conclusion**: FPS performance is **hardware-limited by lighting conditions**, not software bottlenecks.

### Why This Happens

Sony XQ-BT52 camera (like all modern smartphone cameras) automatically adjusts frame rate based on ambient light:

**Low Light Conditions (Desk lamp OFF)**:
- Camera increases **exposure time** to capture more light
- Longer exposure = Lower FPS (~16-17 FPS)
- Trade-off: Better image quality vs lower frame rate

**Good Lighting Conditions (Desk lamp ON)**:
- Camera uses **shorter exposure time**
- Shorter exposure = Higher FPS (28-29 FPS)
- Optimal performance achieved

This is **normal camera behavior**, not a performance issue!
