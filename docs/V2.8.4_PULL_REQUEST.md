## 💡 Key Findings

### 1. BitmapPool Key Explosion Root Cause

**Problem**: `ceil(mapRect())` causing floating-point dimension variations
- Expected: 720x720
- Actual: 721x720, 719x720, 722x720, etc.
- Result: Pool keys exploded from 2 to 10+, hit rate dropped to <50%

**Solution**: Fixed integer dimension calculation based on rotation angle
- Result: Keys 10+ → 1, Hit Rate 99% ✅

### 2. Lighting Affects Camera FPS (Hardware Behavior)

**Discovery**: Initial test showed 16.7 FPS due to insufficient ambient lighting

**Controlled Experiment**:
| Lighting Condition | FPS | Notes |
|-------------------|-----|-------|
| Desk lamp ON | 27-29 FPS | ✅ Optimal performance |
| Desk lamp OFF | 16-17 FPS | ⚠️ Camera auto-throttle |

**Explanation**: Camera hardware automatically reduces frame rate in low light to increase exposure time for better image quality. This is **normal behavior**, not a software issue.

### 3. Pipeline Decoupling Works

**Camera Pipeline**: Runs at 27-29 FPS independently
- YUV Conversion: 4.6ms average
- Transform: 18.3ms average
- Total: ~23ms (supports 27 FPS)

**Analysis Pipeline**: Throttled to 10 FPS via `.sample(100)`
- ML Kit Detection: ~120ms average
- Lighting Analysis: ~28ms average
- Total E2E: ~140ms (doesn't block camera)

**Key Insight**: ML Kit's 120ms processing time doesn't affect camera FPS because analysis runs on background thread at 10 FPS, completely decoupled from camera pipeline.
